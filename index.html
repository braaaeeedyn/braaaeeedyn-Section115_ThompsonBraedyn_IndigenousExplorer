Welcome to Indigenous Explorer

Explore and learn about indigenous populations around the world and the effects of genetic isolation in an engaging and educational way.

Throughout the world, indigenous populations are preservations of genetic diversity and prehistoric ways of life. These populations are found in various corners of the world, from the highest of altitudes, to isolated islands, to within ravenous rainforests. These communties offer insight into the many phenomenons, such as human adaptation, culture, and genetic diversity.
